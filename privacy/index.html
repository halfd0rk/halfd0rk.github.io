Privacy Policy

Effective October 30, 2026

This policy explains what information Half Dork Studios ("we", "us") collects through halfdork.com and the tools we use to run our social media accounts, and how we use it.

What we collect on this website

  • No accounts, no cookies, no analytics. This site does not set cookies or run tracking or advertising scripts.
  • Newsletter. The signup form is not active yet. Anything typed into it is discarded and never sent to us. When the newsletter launches, we will update this policy before collecting any email addresses.
  • Hosting logs. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical data such as your IP address for security and operations. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • Fonts. Text is displayed using Google Fonts, so your browser requests font files from Google. See the Google Privacy Policy.

Social media publishing

We use a self-hosted scheduling tool to publish content to social media accounts that Half Dork Studios owns or manages. When we connect one of our accounts through a platform's official login (such as YouTube, TikTok, Instagram or X), the platform gives our tool access tokens and basic profile information for that account.

  • We use this access only to publish and manage our own posts and to view their basic performance.
  • Tokens and account data are stored on our own server and are not sold, shared or used for advertising.
  • We do not collect data about other people's accounts beyond what the platform shows publicly alongside our own posts.
  • Access can be revoked at any time from the connected platform's security settings, and we delete the related data when an account is disconnected.

Where we use YouTube API Services, the Google Privacy Policy also applies.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We only share information when required by law or to protect our rights and safety.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.
