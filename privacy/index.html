Privacy Policy

Effective October 30, 2026

Half Dork Studios ("we", "us") runs the website halfdork.com and operates a social media scheduling tool at postiz.halfdork.com (the "Service"). This policy explains what information we access, how we use it, and how you can have it deleted.

This website

halfdork.com does not use accounts, cookies, analytics or advertising scripts. The newsletter signup is not active yet; anything typed into it is discarded and never sent to us. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log technical data such as IP addresses (see the GitHub Privacy Statement), and loads fonts from Google Fonts (see the Google Privacy Policy).

Information we access

When you connect a social media account to the Service, the platform asks you to grant us permission. Depending on the platform, we receive: your account or channel name, ID and profile picture; access tokens that let us publish on your behalf; and the posts, videos and basic statistics for content we publish for you. We do not access your password.

YouTube and Google

The Service uses YouTube API Services. By connecting a YouTube channel you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. We use Google data only to upload and manage videos you have asked us to publish, and to show you their status and statistics.

Half Dork Studios' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. You can revoke our access at any time at Google security settings.

How we use information

Only to schedule and publish content you have approved, show you its status, and support you. We do not sell, rent or share your data with anyone, and we do not use it for advertising or to train AI models.

Storage and security

Data is stored on our private server hosted by OVHcloud in Virginia, USA, protected by encrypted (HTTPS) connections, a firewall and restricted access. The Service is not open to the public; sign-ups are disabled.

Retention and deletion

We keep account data only while your account is connected. When you disconnect an account or ask us to delete your data, we delete its tokens and stored data within 30 days. Data received through the YouTube API that we store is refreshed or deleted at least every 30 days.

To request deletion (including for Facebook and Instagram data), email halfdorkstudios@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion" and the account name.

Children

The website and Service are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date at the top shows when it last changed.
